Use these terms consistently. Prefer **team** over workspace or project for the organizational unit.

## team

The organizational unit that owns resources, members, and billing. Resources cannot move between teams. A team cannot be deleted.

## personal team

The team created for you at signup. It is exclusive to a single user. Membership cannot be changed and invitations are disabled.

## collaborative team

Any team that is not a personal team. Owners can invite members by email.

## owner

A member with administrative privileges: team settings, invitations, role changes, status pages, and (if billing admin) billing.

## member

A participant on a team who can work with resources according to product permissions but cannot manage team membership or status-page configuration.

## billing admin

The single owner on a team who is financially responsible for that team. Manages checkout, addons, the Paddle portal, credits, and transfer of the billing admin role.

## resource

Anything manageable under a team, such as a cron job, monitor, assertion, incident, or status page.

## paused resource

A resource held above current [entitlement](/concepts/capacity/). It keeps configuration and history, does not run, and still occupies a slot. Distinct from user-disabled and from abuse suspension.

## addon

A quantity-based recurring purchase that extends one resource type's capacity without changing the base plan. Available on Free, Starter, and Standard.

## credit

Team-scoped balance stored in USD cents. Credits do not expire and cannot move between teams. The billing admin can exchange them into a one-time checkout discount. Credits are not cash.

## assertion

A team-scoped evaluation linked to a monitor. Assertion runs are healthy or failure and, with hysteresis, open or close an incident.

## incident

A team-scoped disruption record, reported by monitoring or by a member. Incidents are not deletable. Status comes from the latest response. Only published incidents can appear on a status page.
