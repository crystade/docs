Every team is always on exactly one plan: Free, Starter, or Standard. Addons extend a single resource type without changing the base plan. Billing rules live in [Billing and plans](/guides/billing/).

## Base plans

| Plan | Price | Best for |
|------|-------|----------|
| **Free** | $0 | Solo developers, side projects, and prototypes |
| **Starter** | $15/month | Small teams launching production workloads |
| **Standard** | $49/month | Growing teams that need more ownership flexibility |

Currency is USD. Payments, taxes, invoices, and payment method updates are handled by Paddle. Paid plans and addons include a 30-day money-back guarantee from the initial purchase date.

## Seats

| Feature | Free | Starter | Standard |
|---------|:----:|:-------:|:--------:|
| Team members | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Owners | 1 | 2 | Unlimited |

## Cron jobs

| Feature | Free | Starter | Standard |
|---------|------|---------|----------|
| Non-deleted jobs | 5 | 50 | 200 |
| Manual triggers per team | 50/week | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Minimum interval | 5m | 1m | 1m |
| Max response body capture | 0 | 4 KB | 16 KB |
| HTTP/1.1 and HTTP/2.0 | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Execution log visibility | 30 days | 90 days | 365 days |
| Retry | No | Yes | Yes |
| Tracing metadata | No | No | Yes |

**Addon:** 10 additional active jobs for $1.99/month. Available on all plans.
