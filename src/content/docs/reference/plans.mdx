## Base Plans

| Plan | Price | Best for |
|------|-------|----------|
| **Free** | $0 | Solo developers, side projects, and prototypes |
| **Starter** | $15/month | Small teams launching production workloads |
| **Standard** | $49/month | Growing teams that need more ownership flexibility |

## Seats

| Feature | Free | Starter | Standard |
|---------|:----:|:-------:|:--------:|
| Team members | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Owners | 1 | 2 | Unlimited |

<Aside>

## Billing Basics

- Currency is USD.
- Payments, taxes, invoices, and payment method updates are handled by Paddle.
- Paid plans and addons include a 30-day money-back guarantee from the initial purchase date.
- Plan downgrades, addon decreases, and cancellations take effect at the next billing date.
