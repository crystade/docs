## Sign In

Create an account with Google or GitHub. After signing in, Crystade creates a personal workspace for you automatically.

<Steps>

1. **Sign in** with Google or GitHub.

2. **Open your personal team.** Use it for solo projects, experiments, or private resources.

3. **Create your first resource.** Add a cron job, health check, or monitor from your team dashboard.

</Steps>

<Aside>
Your personal team is private. If you need collaboration, create a separate team and invite members.
</Aside>

## Work With a Team

Collaborative teams keep resources, member access, and billing grouped together.

<Steps>

1. **Create a team** from the team switcher or dashboard.

2. **Invite teammates** by email.

3. **Assign roles** so trusted teammates can help manage the team.

</Steps>
