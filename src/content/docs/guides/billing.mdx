Crystade billing is scoped to a team. Each team has its own plan, addons, invoices, and billing admin.

Payments, invoices, taxes, and payment method updates are handled securely by Paddle.

## Plans

| Plan | Price | Best for |
|------|-------|----------|
| **Free** | $0 | Solo developers, side projects, and early prototypes |
| **Starter** | $15/month | Small teams launching production workloads |
| **Standard** | $49/month | Growing teams with more resources and owners |

<Aside>
Resource limits vary by resource type. For example, cron job limits are documented with cron jobs rather than in this billing overview.
</Aside>

## Seats and Owners

| Feature | Free | Starter | Standard |
|---------|:----:|:-------:|:--------:|
| Team members | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Owners | 1 | 2 | Unlimited |

## Addons

Addons let you increase capacity for a specific resource without changing your base plan. For example, a team may add more cron job capacity while staying on the same plan.

- Available on all plans, including Free
- Billed monthly
- Managed by the team's billing admin
- Scoped to the team that purchased them

## Plan and Addon Changes

| Change | When it takes effect |
|--------|----------------------|
| Upgrade plan | After successful payment |
| Increase addon quantity | After successful payment |
| Downgrade plan | At the next billing date |
| Decrease addon quantity | At the next billing date |
| Cancel paid subscription | At the end of the current paid period |

<Aside type="tip">
Crystade does not delete resources or execution history when billing changes. If a lower plan or addon quantity reduces capacity, some resources may pause until capacity is available again.
</Aside>

## Invoices and Payment Methods

The billing admin can open the Paddle customer portal to:

- Update payment methods
- View invoices and receipts
- Manage billing details
- Stop future renewal

## Money-Back Guarantee

Paid plans and addons include a 30-day money-back guarantee from the initial purchase date. Contact Crystade support to request a refund within the guarantee window.

## Credits and Discounts

If your team has credits, the billing admin can use them toward eligible checkout discounts. Credits belong to the team and cannot be moved to another team.
