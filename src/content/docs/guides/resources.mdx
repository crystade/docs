## What Is a Resource?

A resource is something your team manages in Crystade, such as a cron job, health check, or monitor.

Resources belong to a single team. This keeps access, billing, and history clear for everyone using the workspace.

## Team-Scoped Resources

When you create a resource, it is attached to the currently selected team.

This means:

- Team members with access can work with that team's resources
- Billing capacity applies to resources in that team
- Resources and history stay organized by workspace

<Aside>
Create separate teams if different projects need separate access control, billing ownership, or operational visibility.
</Aside>

## Capacity

Your team's plan and addons determine how many active resources you can run. Capacity is calculated per resource type.

For example:

- A base plan may include a certain number of active cron jobs
- An addon may increase cron job capacity without changing the base plan
- Other resource types can have their own limits and addons

## What Happens When Capacity Changes?

If your team downgrades a plan or removes an addon, capacity may become lower than your current usage.

Crystade preserves your resources and history. Resources above the new capacity may be paused until capacity is available again.

Paused resources:

- Keep their configuration
- Keep their history
- Do not run while paused
- Can resume when capacity increases again

<Aside type="tip">
To free capacity immediately, delete resources you no longer need or increase capacity through your plan or addons.
</Aside>
