## Teams

A team is a workspace that owns resources such as cron jobs, health checks, and monitors. Resources stay inside the team where they were created.

Crystade supports two team types:

| Team type | Use it for |
|-----------|------------|
| **Personal team** | Solo work, private experiments, and resources that only you manage. |
| **Collaborative team** | Shared production resources and work that multiple teammates manage together. |

<Aside>
Every account starts with a personal team. Create a collaborative team when you need shared access or team billing.
</Aside>

## Roles

Collaborative teams have two main roles:

| Role | What they can do |
|------|------------------|
| **Owner** | Manage team settings, invite members, update roles, and remove members. |
| **Member** | View team membership and work with team resources according to product permissions. |

Owners should be limited to trusted teammates who are responsible for managing access and team settings.

## Invitations

Owners can invite teammates by email. Invited users can accept or decline the invitation before it expires.

Use invitations when you want to:

- Add engineers to manage jobs and monitors
- Bring an operations teammate into the workspace
- Give a billing or team lead visibility into team activity

## Billing Admin

Each team has one billing admin. This person manages subscriptions, payment details, invoices, and billing changes for the team.

The billing admin must be an owner. If billing responsibility changes, transfer the billing admin role to another owner before changing that user's access.

## Good Practices

<CardGrid>
<Card title="Use teams by environment" icon="setting">
	Separate production and staging resources into different teams if they have different ownership or billing needs.
</Card>
<Card title="Keep ownership small" icon="people">
	Grant owner access only to teammates who need to manage team settings or members.
</Card>
<Card title="Review access regularly" icon="magnifier">
	Remove users who no longer need access to shared cron jobs, checks, or monitors.
</Card>
</CardGrid>
